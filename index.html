Questions

Frequently asked

Is the Free plan really free forever?
Yes. Free includes unlimited trade logging, the calendar, core analytics, the notebook, and cloud sync — forever, no card required. Phantom and Shadow add advanced features like AI insights, backtesting replay, and multi-account tracking.
Where is my data stored?
Your journal is stored privately in Supabase (Postgres) and synced across every device you sign in on. Only you can read your trades. Export or delete everything any time.
What markets are supported?
Anything you trade — forex, futures, indices, equities, crypto, options, commodities. The journal is symbol-agnostic. Backtesting replay (Shadow) currently supports indices, forex, and metals.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — paid plans are month-to-month, cancel any time. Your data stays yours, and you keep access to Free forever.
Will my data sync across devices?
Yes. Sign in on any device — desktop, laptop, phone — and your full journal is there in real time.
